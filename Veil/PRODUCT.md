# Veil

Windows desktop palette and experimental Resolve appearance tool, formerly Omen Studio. Python/Tkinter with privately bundled DM Sans. Preserve existing palettes, named HEX import/export, recovery, all Resolve behavior and build/version guards.

Brand authority: the user-supplied LOGO_VEIL.png, retained byte-for-byte as veil-logo-original.png. Use its iridescent V as the app icon and splash mark. Splash proportions remain approximately 2.35:1, at the incumbent 640×273, 800×341 and 960×409 sizes, with three-second display timing. Iridescent satin framing supports the mark; the operating interface remains the established neutral dark/light workspace.

Cherry Blossom remains a separate Resolve theme. Its full native coverage investigation is staged separately and remains unverified. The rebrand makes no new Resolve-theme application or compatibility claim.
