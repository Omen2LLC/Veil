amranthus replaces Colorburst in v0.72. The other 14 palettes are unchanged from v0.71.

The source is lovely-amaranthus-16.hex, preserved here byte-for-byte. The uploaded file contains no author metadata; no creator credit is invented. Source slot order is preserved. Slot 4 becomes Deep Lagoon to broaden a redundant purple family while retaining the floral identity.
