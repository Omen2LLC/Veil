Veil v0.85 — Cherry Blossom integration

Close Veil normally and reopen through your usual launcher, or open Veil/Veil.vbs.
Save your work and close Resolve normally. In Veil, open Themes > Cherry Blossom > Apply Theme.
Apply checks both layers, installs or reuses Cherry Blossom Fusion, and starts a themed Resolve session.
Windows may request administrator approval for the Fusion helper, identified as Python.
To revert, close Resolve normally and choose Restore Original. Native restoration of this expanded candidate still needs verification.

Coverage remains partial: reviewed stylesheet surfaces and Fusion are integrated; custom-painted timelines, node areas, meters and some panels retain native styling. No full-program coverage is claimed.
Original resources, palette data and recovery guards are retained. Your ordinary Resolve shortcut is unchanged.
